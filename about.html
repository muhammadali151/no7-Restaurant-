How it started

Seven tables, one idea

In the winter of 2014, Elena Rossi and Marco Bianchi signed the lease on a narrow, slightly crooked unit at number 7, MM Alam Road, Lahore. There was room for seven tables, an open kitchen the size of a galley, and not much else.

The idea was simple and a little stubborn: cook what's good that week, write it on a board, pour wine made by people who care, and treat every guest like they'd been invited for supper.

Word travelled the way it does in a neighbourhood — slowly, then all at once. Within a year the board was full every night.

What guides us

Ingredients first, always

We don't believe in long menus or complicated plates. We believe in a great carrot, a properly aged steak, a loaf baked that morning. Most of what we serve travels fewer miles than our guests do.

Our relationships with growers, fishers and winemakers are the real recipe. Some have been with us since the first week; a few now name a vintage after the restaurant.

Everything that can be made in-house, is — the bread, the pasta, the ice cream, the bitters behind the bar.
