1. Introduction

No. 7 Bistro & Wine Bar ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or make a reservation, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

  • Reservation details — name, phone number, email address, party size, date and time, and any dietary notes you provide.
  • Contact form submissions — name, email, and the message content you send us.
  • Usage data — basic analytics such as pages visited and browser type, used to improve the site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to confirm and manage bookings, respond to enquiries, and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We only share information with trusted service providers who help us operate (for example, booking or email systems), and only to the extent necessary for them to provide that service.

5. Data Retention

We retain reservation and enquiry records only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected, or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us using the details below.

7. Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies to ensure the site functions correctly. We do not use cookies for third-party advertising.

8. Contact Us
