Who we are

Cooking that tastes of where it comes from

No. 7 began as a seven-table room with an open kitchen and a short, hand-written menu. A decade on, little has changed about the idea: we buy from growers and fishers we know, cook over fire and in cast iron, and let a few good ingredients speak for themselves.

Our wine list leans towards small, independent and low-intervention producers — the kind of bottles our sommelier would happily drink on her night off.

— Elena & Marco, founders
